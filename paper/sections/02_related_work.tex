\section{Related work and existing practice}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Existing practice}
WHO estimates that 10.5\% of medical products in low- and middle-income countries fail quality tests \cite{who2017oneinten}, and a later review found that most failures are substandard rather than falsified \cite{ozawa2022characterizing}. A substandard batch from a licensed maker looks correct in every respect, so packaging cues do not help. In India, laboratories tested 116,323 samples in 2024--25 and found 3,104 not of standard quality and 245 spurious or adulterated \cite{roy2025fiveyears}. Until mid-2025 a person who wanted to check a pack had to open the monthly PDF lists and search them by hand; since August 2025 the lists sit in a month-by-month web viewer \cite{cdsco2025nsqlink}. Schedule H2 QR codes are assigned by each manufacturer and are mandatory for 300 brands \cite{cdsco2023faqqr}. They confirm the code and carry no test result, and rogue verification services have answered every query positively \cite{who2021traceability}. Field screening devices cost from US\$3 to over US\$60,000 and few have field evidence \cite{vickers2018field,kovacs2014technologies}, and a review of 25 medicine-verification apps found none that checked a regulator's quality list \cite{ciapponi2021mobile}.

Several open tools built on CDSCO's data appeared in 2025 and 2026. Blacklisted offers typed batch lookup with an email digest \cite{blacklisted2026}. Nazar reads QR codes and the printed batch line from a photo and returns ``can't tell'' when it sees look-alike characters \cite{rastogi2026nazar}. SafeDose marks a batch hit with a different drug name as ``caution'' \cite{shaurya2026safedose}, and RecallOps India matches alerts against distributor stock with a human review queue \cite{ibnweb32026recallops}. Among the tools we found (searched October 2026), none asks the person to resolve a doubtful character during a check, corroborates product, brand, dates and maker on a consumer pack, or runs fully offline. StripCheck adds these, together with a verifier agent.

\subsection{Reading codes on packs}
On 27 pharmaceutical pack images, a region-based detector reached an F1 score of 80.9 against 47.1 for Tesseract \cite{koponen2024novel}. StripCheck uses the PP-OCR model family \cite{du2020ppocr,li2022ppocrv3} through RapidOCR \cite{rapidai2021rapidocr}. Confidence scores of modern networks are poorly calibrated \cite{guo2017calibration}, and on degraded text vision-language models fall back on language priors \cite{he2025seeing}, so a fluent reading of a code is no evidence that the code is right. Contrast enhancement did not improve licence-plate reading \cite{tavares2024comparison}, consistent with our observation that an enhanced re-read repeats a confident misread.

\subsection{Post-OCR correction and hotlist matching}
Noisy-channel models rank candidate corrections by how likely the observed characters are given each candidate \cite{kernighan1990spelling,brill2000improved}, and a channel model over a closed vocabulary cut OCR word error by 70\% \cite{kolak2002ocr}. Routing only disputed words to people raised accuracy to 99.1\% \cite{vonahn2008recaptcha}, the pattern behind StripCheck's character questions. Licence-plate recognition is the closest operational analogue: its confusable pairs (B/8, D/0, I/1, S/5, Z/2) match those on batch codes \cite{laroca2021efficient}, about one hotlist hit in five was a misread in one city audit \cite{seattleoig2023alpr}, and a misread of 3 as 7 led to a stop at gunpoint, after which the court stressed visual verification before acting \cite{green2014ninth}. StripCheck's confirmation gate applies the same rule to medicine alarms.

\subsection{Record linkage}
Fellegi and Sunter sort record pairs into links, non-links and possible links that need manual review \cite{fellegi1969theory}. StripCheck maps these zones to an alarm, \NLISTED{} and a question or abstention. Errors in a blocking key lose true matches, so practical systems use several blocking passes \cite{winkler2006overview,christen2012survey}; confusion variants of the batch key play that role, and their candidate recall can be reported separately from final accuracy \cite{papadakis2020survey}. Language models match entities nearly as well as fine-tuned matchers but are sensitive to the prompt \cite{peeters2025entity}, which supports a deterministic matcher. Indian brand names collide across unrelated molecules \cite{neelakantan2024lookalike}, so a brand match alone never raises an alarm.

\subsection{LLM agents and external verification}
Agents that interleave reasoning and tool use \cite{yao2023react,schick2023toolformer} and that critique their own output \cite{shinn2023reflexion,madaan2023selfrefine} are common, but models cannot reliably correct their own reasoning without external feedback \cite{huang2024cannot}, and planning works best when an external verifier checks the model's proposals \cite{kambhampati2024llmmodulo}. Agents completed under half of the tasks in $\tau$-bench \cite{yao2024taubench}, practitioner guidance favours simple, composable workflows over complex frameworks \cite{schluntz2024building}, and agent comparisons should report cost against simple baselines \cite{kapoor2024agents}. Small models suit narrow agent tasks \cite{belcak2025slm}. StripCheck therefore lets a small model choose only among allowed actions, keeps a deterministic planner as fallback, and checks every draft with hard rules.

\subsection{Abstention and risk communication}
The reject option \cite{chow1970reject} and selective classification \cite{geifman2017selective} formalise answering only when confident. Language models rarely abstain on medical questions \cite{cocchieri2026abstain}, so StripCheck's abstention and wording come from rules and templates. People over-rely on automated advice \cite{parasuraman2010complacency}, and cognitive forcing functions reduce this \cite{bucinca2021trust}, which is why the confirmation asks the person to type specific characters from the pack. Adding a residual-risk sentence to a negative test result raised correct understanding from 54\% to 89\% \cite{batteux2022residual}, which supports the wording of \NLISTED. Consumer recall lookups elsewhere state how current they are \cite{ecfr2026vin}, a convention StripCheck adopts.
